Legal notices are designed for courts and lawyers, not for the person opening the envelope. Our **Notice Scanner** (shipping after public beta) combines OCR, structure detection, and careful AI prompting to produce:

1. **Type of notice** — e.g. demand letter, summons, eviction, tax, consumer.
2. **Deadlines and amounts** — extracted where they appear in the text.
3. **Plain-language summary** — what it likely means in context (not legal advice).
4. **Suggested next steps** — document, respond, seek counsel — always with a CTA to a **verified lawyer** for your case.

### Why this is not “auto legal advice”

The model does not know your full facts or local court practice. It **guides**; a qualified advocate **decides**. That is a product rule, not a disclaimer in the footer.
