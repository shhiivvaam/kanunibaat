Most Indians never speak to a lawyer until something has already gone wrong — a notice on the door, a salary stopped, a message that scares you at midnight. By then, fear and jargon make every step harder.

**Jurisly is not here to fix the courts.** We are here to help you **navigate** them: plain-language answers, tools to understand notices, and a path to **verified advocates** when you need a human.

### What you can expect from this blog

- Practical explainers tied to real situations (consumer, rent, employment, family).
- Product updates as we ship notice scanning, emergency guides, and consultations.
- Honest boundaries: when AI can inform and when you must call a lawyer.
